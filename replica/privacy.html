Privacy Policy

Information We Collect

The Societas Magica collects contact information and descriptive data about its members. This information is obtained through the membership application process and through the member profile section of the members-only area of this website.

How We Use This Information

Member information is used to communicate directly with members and to provide information exchange and other services to the membership. Member details are accessible to other members in the restricted area of the website and may support legitimate professional activities — for example, identifying potential speakers, contributors to publications, or session organizers.

Members retain complete authority over what information is made visible to other members. You may log into your account at any time to review and update the information displayed in your profile.

Third-Party Sharing

The Societas Magica never supplies the information it collects to third parties for any reason. We encourage all members to uphold this commitment to privacy in their own use of member contact details obtained through the website.

Member Control

Members may log into the website to identify exactly what information is made available to other members, and to update or restrict that information at any time. If you wish to have your information removed from the membership database, contact the Website Administrator at admin@societasmagica.org.

Cookies

This website uses cookies to maintain login sessions. Cookies are required for the member area to function. By logging in, you consent to the use of session cookies for this purpose. No tracking or advertising cookies are used.

Contact

For questions about this privacy policy or about your personal data, contact the Website Administrator: admin@societasmagica.org
