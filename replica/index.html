Welcome to the Societas Magica.

The Societas Magica exists for communication and exchange among scholars interested in the study of magic. We welcome scholars whose interests touch on magic in any context — whether the interest arises from the positive side (occultism, paganism, and so on) or from the negative (sorcery trials, condemnation accounts, and so on).

The interests of the membership include the history and sociology of magic; theological and intellectual apprehensions of magic; practices and theories of magic; and objects, artifacts, and texts either qualified as magical by their creators or forming the substance of an accusation of magic by others.

About the Societas

The Societas Magica was founded in 1994 by Richard Kieckhefer, Claire Fanger, and Robert Mathiesen, originally to sponsor sessions on the history of magic in the Middle Ages at the International Congress on Medieval Studies at Kalamazoo, Michigan. The activities of the Societas have expanded considerably with its increasingly diverse membership, and our interests are not limited to the medieval period.

The Societas continues to sponsor sessions both at the Medieval Congress at Kalamazoo and at other academic conferences as proposed and organized by members. We publish a newsletter twice yearly and maintain affiliated publications through Penn Press and Penn State University Press.
