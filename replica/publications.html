Publications

The Societas Magica maintains close affiliations with two major scholarly publishing ventures in the history of magic: a peer-reviewed journal and an academic book series, both produced through Pennsylvania-based university presses.

Magic, Ritual, & Witchcraft

Magic, Ritual, & Witchcraft is a peer-reviewed scholarly journal published by the University of Pennsylvania Press. It was established in 2006 and publishes two issues per year. The journal covers the history and anthropology of magic, ritual, and witchcraft across all historical periods and geographic regions, with an emphasis on rigorous, interdisciplinary scholarship.

The journal welcomes submissions from scholars working across disciplines — history, religious studies, anthropology, literary studies, art history, and related fields — on subjects from antiquity to the present day.

  • Publisher: University of Pennsylvania Press
  • First issue: Spring 2006
  • Frequency: Two issues per year
  • Member benefit: Societas Magica members may subscribe at a discounted rate when paying annual dues

Magic in History Book Series

The Magic in History series is sponsored by the Societas Magica and published by Penn State University Press. It has been active since 1998. The series explores the role that magic and the occult have played in European culture, religion, science, and politics from antiquity to the modern period.

Titles in the series include editions of important primary texts, translations of key works, and significant new research monographs. The series brings cultural, literary, and social-historical perspectives to bear on the history of the magical arts.

  • Publisher: Penn State University Press
  • Series editors: Claire Fanger (Rice University) and Richard Kieckhefer (Northwestern University)
  • Active since: 1998
  • Member discount: 20% off all titles in the series
Member discount: Societas Magica members receive a 20% discount on all Magic in History titles. Contact the Treasurer at bookkeeper@societasmagica.org to obtain the current discount code.

Scholars interested in submitting a monograph or edition proposal to the Magic in History series are welcome to query the editors about suitability before submitting a full proposal.

The Societas Magica Newsletter

The Societas Magica Newsletter has been published since 1995 and appears twice yearly. It carries lead articles on current research, shorter notes and queries, dissertation notices, conference reports, and announcements of new publications. The newsletter is distributed to all members and is archived on this website.
