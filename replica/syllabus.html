Syllabus Project

The Societas Magica Syllabus Project is a repository of course syllabi for courses on magic and the occult offered at colleges and universities. It is maintained by Amelia Carr at Allegheny College. The project makes it easier for scholars developing new courses to see how colleagues have approached the subject, and encourages the teaching of magic studies at the undergraduate and graduate levels.

To submit a syllabus for inclusion in the project, or for questions about the collection, contact: acarr@allegheny.edu

Submitting a Syllabus

We welcome syllabi for any course dealing substantially with magic, witchcraft, ritual, the occult, or related subjects, at any level (undergraduate or graduate) and from any disciplinary perspective (history, religious studies, anthropology, literature, etc.).

Please send syllabi as PDF or Word documents to acarr@allegheny.edu with a brief note identifying the course level and institutional context.

Using This Resource

Syllabi in the collection may be used freely for educational and course-development purposes. Please credit the original instructor if adapting materials from a shared syllabus. The project does not reproduce syllabi publicly online; contact Amelia Carr to request access to specific courses.
