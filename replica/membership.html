Who May Join

Membership in the Societas Magica is open to any scholar with a professional interest in magic studies. We welcome academics at all career stages, independent scholars, and researchers whose work touches on magic in any historical, geographic, or disciplinary context.

The Societas reserves the right to exclude from membership individuals whose behavior or conduct reflects badly on the organization or on the scholarly community it serves.

Member Benefits

  • Access to the members-only directory — searchable by name, institution, and research interest
  • Subscription to the Societas Magica Newsletter (two issues per year)
  • 20% discount on all titles in the Magic in History series (Penn State University Press)
  • Option to subscribe to Magic, Ritual, & Witchcraft (University of Pennsylvania Press) at a member-discounted rate
  • Access to the members-only email list for announcements and professional exchange
  • Eligibility for the Travel Bursary to support conference presentations
  • Right to propose and organize conference sessions under the Societas Magica banner

Member Contributions

As a scholarly community, the Societas relies on member participation. Full members are encouraged to contribute through:

  • Refereeing manuscripts for the newsletter or affiliated publications
  • Contributing articles, notes, and queries to the newsletter
  • Circulating notices of relevant research, dissertations, and manuscript discoveries
  • Organizing or participating in conference sessions sponsored by the Societas

Dues & Payment

Annual dues support the newsletter, the website, and the Societas's conference activities. Dues are collected via PayPal. You do not need a PayPal account — payment by credit card is also accepted through the PayPal interface.

Note on journal subscription: When paying dues, you will have the option to include a discounted subscription to Magic, Ritual, & Witchcraft. Your mailing address for the journal must be provided separately — your PayPal address is not used for this purpose.

To pay dues, click the PayPal button below. For questions about membership or payment, contact the Treasurer at bookkeeper@societasmagica.org.
