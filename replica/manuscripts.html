Manuscript Resources

The following resources are compiled for scholars working with primary manuscripts in the history of magic. This page lists major searchable databases, digital libraries, and specialist collections relevant to the field.

For questions about manuscript resources or to suggest additions to this page, contact the Manuscripts coordinator at manuscripts@societasmagica.org (Claire Fanger, Rice University).

Featured Collections

Frank Klaassen's Magic Manuscripts

A curated collection of manuscripts compiled by Frank Klaassen, focusing on ritual and learned magic in the medieval and early modern periods. An invaluable specialist resource for the field.

John of Morigny's Liber florum celestis doctrine

The Liber florum celestis doctrine (Book of the Flowers of Heavenly Teaching) is a fourteenth-century visionary and liturgical magic text of exceptional interest. Online resources and manuscript witnesses are available for scholars working on this text.

Manuscript Databases

  • British Library Manuscript Catalogue
    Comprehensive catalogue of manuscripts held at the British Library, London. One of the largest and most important collections for medieval magic research.
  • Hill Monastic Manuscript Library (HMML)
    Database of microfilmed and digitized manuscripts from monastic and ecclesiastical libraries worldwide. Contains between 30,000 and 90,000 entries.
  • Jordanus: Catalogue of Medieval Scientific Manuscripts
    Specialist catalogue of medieval scientific manuscripts, maintained by the Bayerische Akademie der Wissenschaften. Covers natural philosophy, mathematics, astronomy, and related subjects including magic.
  • Manuscripta Medievalia
    German manuscript database with extensive holdings in medieval Latin and vernacular texts. Between 30,000 and 90,000 entries.
  • Adam McLean's List of Alchemical Manuscripts
    A specialist hand-compiled list of manuscripts containing alchemical texts, maintained by Adam McLean. A useful finding aid for scholars of alchemy.

Digital Manuscript Libraries

  • Bibliothèque Nationale de France — Gallica
    An increasing repository of digitized manuscripts from the BnF's extraordinary medieval holdings. Full-image access to many key texts in the history of magic.
  • Folger Shakespeare Library — Book of Magic Collection
    Digitized magical manuscripts and printed books from the Folger's rich early modern holdings in Washington, D.C.
  • e-codices — Virtual Manuscript Library of Switzerland
    High-quality digital facsimiles of Swiss medieval manuscripts, with excellent search and browse tools.
  • Oxford Digital Facsimiles
    Digital facsimiles of selected manuscripts from Oxford University's Bodleian Library and college libraries.
  • National Library of Wales — Digital Gallery (Manuscripts)
    Digitized manuscripts from the National Library of Wales, including Welsh-language and Latin materials.
  • The Warburg Institute — Digital Collections
    Digital resources from the Warburg Institute Library, a premier centre for the study of the classical tradition, magic, and the occult in European culture.
  • Princeton Digital Library of Islamic Manuscripts
    Digitized Islamic manuscripts from Princeton's collections, relevant for scholars of Arabic and Persian magical traditions.
  • Digital Dead Sea Scrolls
    High-resolution images of the Dead Sea Scrolls, hosted by the Israel Museum. Relevant for scholars of ancient Jewish magical and liturgical texts.
  • Oxyrhynchus Papyri Database
    Database of the Oxyrhynchus Papyri, including magical and ritual texts from Roman Egypt. Hosted by Oxford.
  • Universität Leipzig — 18th-Century Magic Manuscripts
    Digitized eighteenth-century magical manuscripts from the University of Leipzig library collections.

Other Resources

  • Dee's Spirit Actions
    Digital resource for John Dee's angelic or Enochian diaries and spirit actions, relevant for early modern magical history.
  • Études Magiques
    A French-language resource for ancient magic scholarship, with journal references and bibliographic tools.
