Conferences

The Societas Magica sponsors academic sessions at conferences on magic and related subjects. The organization's primary venue is the International Congress on Medieval Studies at Kalamazoo, Michigan, where we typically organize eight or more sessions each year. Members are also encouraged to propose sessions at other conferences.

To propose a session or inquire about Societas-sponsored sessions, contact the Session Organizer at sessions@societasmagica.org.

Proposing a Session

Members are welcome to propose sessions at any academic conference, not only Kalamazoo. The Societas can lend its name and organizational support to sessions relevant to the study of magic across any period or region. Contact the Session Organizer well in advance of the conference's proposal deadline:

sessions@societasmagica.org — David Porreca, University of Waterloo

For the Kalamazoo Congress specifically, the call for proposals typically opens in the late summer/early fall for sessions the following May. Check the Congress website for the current deadline.
