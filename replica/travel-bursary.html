Travel Bursary

The Societas Magica offers a Travel Bursary to support members who are presenting papers at academic conferences. The bursary is available for presentations at any conference — not only the annual Kalamazoo Congress — provided the paper is relevant to the interests of the Societas Magica (i.e., deals substantially with magic, witchcraft, ritual, or related subjects in any historical period or geographical region).

Current status: The Travel Bursary is currently active and accepting applications. For questions about the current funding level or deadlines, contact the Treasurer.

Eligibility

  • You must be a current member of the Societas Magica with dues paid up to date
  • You must be presenting a paper (not simply attending) at an academic conference
  • The paper must deal with a subject relevant to the interests of the Societas Magica
  • The conference can be at any level — international, national, or regional
  • Graduate students and early-career scholars are especially encouraged to apply

How to Apply

To apply for the Travel Bursary, send the following information to the Treasurer at bookkeeper@societasmagica.org:

  1. Your name and institutional affiliation
  2. The name and dates of the conference
  3. The title of your paper
  4. A brief abstract of your presentation (150–300 words)
  5. A brief statement of why you are seeking the bursary (1–2 sentences)

Applications are considered on a rolling basis. There is no fixed deadline, but applicants are encouraged to apply as early as possible, as funds are limited and awarded in the order received.
