Newsletter Archive

The Societas Magica Newsletter has been published since Fall 1995. After a hiatus from 2017 to 2020 (due to the absence of a volunteer editor), publication resumed with Issue 36 in Fall 2021 and continues at two issues per year.

All issues are available as PDF downloads. To submit articles, notes, or queries for a future issue, contact the Newsletter Editor at newsletters@societasmagica.org. Lead articles should be 1,500–2,500 words.
